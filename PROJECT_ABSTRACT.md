# **Cakesman Bakery: An Integrated E-Commerce Platform for Custom Cake Ordering and Management**

---

## **Area/Domain of Project:**
E-Commerce, Bakery Management System, Web Application Development, Customer Experience Optimization

---

## **ABSTRACT ABOUT THE PROJECT:**

The bakery industry has traditionally relied on phone orders, walk-in customers, and manual inventory management, resulting in inefficient operations, poor customer experience, and limited market reach. Traditional bakeries struggle with order management, customization tracking, scheduling coordination, and delivery logistics, creating bottlenecks that prevent growth and customer satisfaction. The existing market gap involves the lack of comprehensive, user-friendly digital platforms specifically designed for bakeries that seamlessly integrate product catalog management, real-time order processing, dynamic pricing, and customer authentication while maintaining the ability to handle complex customizations such as flavor selection, egg preferences, and specialized dietary requirements.

Cakesman Bakery addresses these critical challenges by developing a full-stack e-commerce platform that revolutionizes how customers order custom cakes and how bakery operators manage their business. The platform combines an intuitive React-based frontend with a robust Node.js backend, enabling customers to browse products, customize orders with dynamic pricing, schedule deliveries or pickups, and track their orders in real-time. The system implements advanced features including flavor customization stations, egg option preferences, delivery fee calculations with discount thresholds, and comprehensive cart management. For bakery operators, the solution provides order management dashboards, inventory tracking, and scheduling optimization. This digital transformation is crucial for modernizing the bakery business model, expanding customer reach through online channels, reducing manual errors, improving operational efficiency, and ultimately increasing profitability while delivering exceptional customer experiences that drive loyalty and business growth in an increasingly digital marketplace.
